Privacy in this prototype

Records are stored in localStorage in your browser, not submitted to a Rentun backend or AI provider. This build has no analytics, cookies, or external fonts. A future host may process access logs when serving the website.

Delete individual records with Delete, or clear site data to remove all records. Export first. Anyone with access to your browser profile may access these records. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

Prototype terms and limitations

This is an evaluation prototype without service-level or retention guarantees. Export is a downloadable record, not an import workflow. Maintain your own backups. Rentun does not send messages or make decisions for you. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude is a planned integration only.
